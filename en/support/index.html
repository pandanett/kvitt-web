Help and contact

Contact

Write to kvitt.app@outlook.com. Kvitt is made by one person, so I answer when I can.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Kvitt's version (at the bottom of Settings) and what happened. Preferably do not send receipts with personal data; if one is needed, you can hide your name and address first.

Common questions

The total or the date was read wrong.

Open the purchase and correct the field. Fields the app is unsure of are marked with «!» when you add the receipt. If you scanned it, «Take a new picture» with better light and the receipt flat on a table may help.

How do I keep my data safe?

Kvitt does not sync. Use «Save a copy elsewhere …» in Settings and save the copy in iCloud Drive or on a Mac. The data is also included in your phone's normal iCloud backup, if it is turned on.

I have a new phone.

If you restore the new phone from your iCloud backup, Kvitt comes along. Otherwise: install Kvitt and choose «Restore from file …» in Settings with a copy you have saved.

Can I use Kvitt on several devices?

Not in this version. The data lives on one phone.

How do I delete everything?

«Delete all data» in Settings deletes purchases and receipts. A backup is taken first, so you can undo. To remove the backups too, tick «Also delete the backups in the app (cannot be undone)». Deleting the app removes everything.

The scanner shows «Give Kvitt access to the camera in Settings».

Go to Settings → Kvitt on your phone and turn on Camera.

Does Kvitt send my receipts anywhere?

No. See the privacy policy.
