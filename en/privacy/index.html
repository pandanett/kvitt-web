Kvitt privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 · version 1

Kvitt is an app for keeping receipts and warranties. It is built so your data stays on your phone.

In short

  • You do not need an account.
  • Receipts, purchases and pictures are stored on your phone and never sent to me.
  • I collect nothing about you: no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • The only thing the app fetches from the internet is exchange rates from Norges Bank (the central bank of Norway).

Who is responsible

Kvitt is made by Nicklas Astrup, as a private individual. Contact: kvitt.app@outlook.com

What the app stores, and where

Kvitt stores your purchases, the receipt files (PDFs and pictures) and what the app reads from them, such as store, date, amounts, items and warranties. A receipt may contain personal data such as your name, address and order number.

All of this is kept in the app's own folder on your phone. It is not sent to me or to any server. Receipts are read on the phone, including text recognition in pictures.

What is never collected

Kvitt has no account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no third-party SDKs that collect data. I cannot see who uses the app or what you keep in it.

The only thing the app sends: exchange rates

When a purchase is in a different currency from the one you show amounts in, Kvitt fetches daily rates from Norges Bank's open service (data.norges-bank.no). The request contains only the currency code and the dates, never anything from your receipts. As with any request on the internet, Norges Bank sees the IP address your phone uses. Without a connection the app works as before, just without new rates.

Camera and photos

Kvitt uses the camera only when you scan a receipt, and sees only the pictures and files you choose. The scan is processed on the phone and saved as a receipt in the app.

Backups and copies

Kvitt does not sync between devices. The app's data is included in your phone's normal backup (iCloud or a Mac) if you use one; that backup is handled by Apple under your settings there. If you choose «Save a copy elsewhere …», a copy is saved where you decide, for example iCloud Drive.

Links out of the app

If you tap a store's website or the help and privacy pages, they open in the browser, and that site's privacy policy applies. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses. The pages have no analytics or cookies from me.

If you contact me

If you email kvitt.app@outlook.com, I get your email address and what you write. I use it only to answer you and delete it when the matter is closed. Please do not send receipts with personal data unless it is needed.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have turned on «Share With App Developers» in iOS, Apple may give me anonymous crash reports. They contain technical information about the crash, not the content you have stored in the app. You control this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

How to delete your data

  • «Delete all data» in Kvitt's settings deletes all purchases and receipt files. The app takes a backup first, so you can undo.
  • If you tick «Also delete the backups in the app (cannot be undone)», all backups inside the app are deleted as well, and none of your data is left in Kvitt.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything, including the backups inside the app.
  • Copies you have saved elsewhere, and your phone's own backup, are deleted where they are kept.

Children

Kvitt is not made for children and collects nothing about anyone.

Your rights

I hold no data about you beyond emails you send me yourself, so there is little to access or delete on my side; you are in full control in the app. If you ask, I delete your emails. If you have questions, write to kvitt.app@outlook.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Norway: Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no).

Changes

If what the app does with data changes, this page is updated with a new date and version before the change reaches the app.
